Hordelane support

Hordelane: Zombie Survival — published by Roy Greenberg

Contact: email playrg.games@gmail.com with a short description of the problem, your device model (for example "iPhone 14 Pro" or "Galaxy A06") and the game version shown in Settings. We answer in English, usually within two working days.

Purchases

  • Something you bought did not arrive. Close the game completely and open it again while connected to the internet: a purchase that was paid for is delivered when the store confirms it, even on a later launch. If it still has not arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the item's name.
  • A purchase shows "waiting for approval" or "not complete yet". The store is holding it - for example a parent's approval (Ask to Buy on iPhone) or a payment that is still being completed. It arrives by itself once the store completes it; there is no need to buy it again.
  • New phone or reinstall on iPhone. Open Settings in the game and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Survivors, weapons, the extra boost place and the current season's HORDE PASS PREMIUM come back. Coins, hearts and HORDE PASS tiers are used up when delivered and stay with the progress on the old install.
  • New phone or reinstall on Android. Survivors, weapons and the extra boost place come back automatically from Google Play. Progress and coins come back only if you were signed in to Google Play Games, which keeps a backup.
  • Refunds are handled by the store: on iPhone at reportaproblem.apple.com, on Android through Google Play's order history. Our Terms of Service explain the details.

Your data

  • What the game collects, and the choice you have about it, is in the Privacy Policy.
  • To delete the gameplay statistics recorded for you, follow Delete your data.
