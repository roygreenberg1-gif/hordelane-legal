Privacy Policy for Hordelane

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Hordelane: Zombie Survival ("Hordelane", "the game") is developed and published by Roy Greenberg. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and how to switch it off. It applies to the Android application with the package name com.greenberg.hordelane.

The short version

The game collects anonymous gameplay statistics — which level you played, whether you won, and what you upgraded — so that the difficulty and pacing can be improved. It does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location, or anything else that identifies you personally. You can turn the collection off at any time inside the game.

What the game collects

What the game does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind.
  • No advertising identifier. Advertising ID collection is switched off.
  • No precise or GPS location.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera access.
  • No text you type. The game has no chat and no user-generated content.
  • The game requests no Android runtime permissions.

The app instance identifier

The identifier is generated randomly when you install the game. It is not your device's serial number, not your advertising ID, and is not linked to any account. It cannot be used to identify you, and it changes if you uninstall and reinstall the game or clear its data.

How to turn it off

Open Settings inside the game and tap GAME DATA until it reads OFF. Collection stops immediately and nothing further is sent. The choice is remembered. The game is fully playable with it off — no feature, reward, or level is withheld.

Purchases

When in-app purchases are available, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives, sees, or stores your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail. Google tells the game only whether a purchase succeeded.

The game records that a purchase happened — which pack, its price tier, and when — as an anonymous analytics event, so that pricing and pacing can be judged. That record carries the same random DATA ID as everything else and contains no payment information and no name.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. The Terms of Service explain what a purchase gives you.

Who processes the data

Analytics are processed by Google Firebase Analytics on the developer's behalf. Google acts as a data processor and is bound by the Firebase Data Processing Terms. See also Firebase privacy information.

The data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. It is used only to improve this game.

Your progress stays on your device

Your saved game — level reached, coins, upgrades, settings, and reward timers — is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded. It is not readable by the developer. Uninstalling the game removes it.

How long it is kept

In the Firebase Analytics reporting console, event data is retained for 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

A copy of the raw, anonymous event records is also exported to Google BigQuery, where it is kept only for as long as it is useful for improving the game, so that changes can be compared over time. These records contain only the gameplay information described above and the random app instance identifier — no name, account, contact details, or advertising identifier. Records are deleted on request, and in any case when they are no longer useful for improving the game.

Your rights, and how to actually use them

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data relating to you, and to object to its processing.

Anonymous data creates a practical problem for those rights: without something to match on, no one can tell which records are yours. The game therefore shows you a DATA ID — a random 12-character tag created on your device when you first play. Open Settings and it is printed on the line under the title; tap that line to copy it.

To have your data deleted, email the address below and include your DATA ID. Records carrying that tag will be deleted. Please send the request before uninstalling: the tag is stored only on your device, and once the game is removed it cannot be recovered by you or by the developer.

To stop collection without deleting anything, switch GAME DATA to OFF in Settings.

If you are in California

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, the categories of personal information collected are identifiers (the random app instance identifier and the DATA ID) and internet or other electronic network activity (the gameplay events listed above). They are collected directly from the app on your device, for the business purpose of analysing and improving the game.

Your personal information is not sold, and it is not shared for cross-context behavioural advertising. The developer has never done either and does not intend to.

You have the right to know what is collected, to request deletion, to request correction, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. The game is identical whether or not you opt out. To make a request, follow the DATA ID instructions above.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom

The developer is the data controller. The legal basis for processing gameplay analytics is consent, which you may withdraw at any time by switching GAME DATA off in Settings; withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. Data is processed by Google on servers that may be located outside the EEA, under Google's standard contractual clauses.

Children

Hordelane is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in Google Play's Families programme. The game does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

Advertising and in-app purchases are planned for a later release, and both involve additional data collection. This policy and the Data Safety declaration on Google Play will both be updated before any such version is published. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy, and requests to delete data, can be sent to playrg.games@gmail.com. Please include your DATA ID with a deletion request.
