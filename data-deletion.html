Delete your Hordelane data

Hordelane: Zombie Survival — published by Roy Greenberg

The gameplay statistics Hordelane records carry no name, no email address, no contact details and no advertising identifier. They are pseudonymous: gameplay information — which stage was played, how it ended, how long it took — tied to a random DATA ID rather than to you, so that a deletion request can be matched to the right records. Those records are what the request below deletes.

Your entries on the global Survival boards, and your invite records from INVITE & GET, are kept under a different random identifier, the BOARD ID, and are removed with that one — see your entries on the global boards and your invite records. The two IDs are separate: the DATA ID does not reach the boards or the invites, and the BOARD ID does not reach the statistics.

Backing your progress up to Google Play Games (Android) is a separate, optional sign-in, and the only part of the game that uses an account of yours; the boards and invites use an anonymous one the game creates by itself. The game is fully playable without it. That backup is not covered by either request, because it is not ours to delete — see your Play Games backup.

How to request deletion of your statistics

  1. Open Hordelane on your device.
  2. Tap the AUDIO / settings button in the lobby.
  3. Find the line reading DATA ID, followed by a random 12-character code such as A5514E11EEA5. Tap it — the code is copied to your clipboard and the screen confirms it. The code is yours alone and appears nowhere else.
  4. Paste it into an email to playrg.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data.

Nothing else is needed. You do not have to explain why, and there is nothing to prove — the DATA ID is the only thing that connects the records to your copy of the game.

On iPhone the steps are the same; the DATA ID is shown only while GAME DATA FULL is on, and switching to GAME DATA OFF stops the statistics at once (the setting reads "no stats sent"). It does not reach the global boards: from the iPhone version that adds them, a Survival result goes to them under your BOARD ID whichever setting is on, and an earlier iPhone version sends nothing to them. There is no Google Play Games backup on iPhone.
Prefer to stop being identified instead of deleting? The same settings screen shows GAME DATA FULL or ANONYMOUS ONLY, with a CHANGE button under it (in earlier versions of the game, tap the mode itself). Tapping it to switch to anonymous only takes effect immediately: the game keeps sending gameplay statistics, but with no DATA ID and no other identifier, so no statistic from then on can be linked to you. You can do both: switch, and still ask for what was already recorded under your DATA ID to be deleted. Copy the DATA ID before you switch; the settings line stops showing it once you are anonymous. The switch covers the statistics only, not the global boards.

How long it takes

Requests are handled by one person, so allow a few days for a reply. There are two copies of the records, and they are honest to separate because only one of them is ours to promise a date for.

The exported analytics tables. This is the copy the developer holds, and it is the one the reports are actually read from. Your records are removed from it within 30 days of the request, and you will get an email confirming it is done.

Google's own reporting console. Deletion there is a request submitted to Google, not something the developer performs. Google states that such a request takes between 7 and 63 days to process, and that data must be more than twelve days old before it can be deleted at all. The request is submitted the same day yours arrives; the schedule after that is Google's, which is why this page does not put a date on it.

Records are deleted automatically no later than 24 months after they were collected, and sooner on request.

Your entries on the global boards

When a Survival run that cleared at least one wave ends, the game enters its result on the public HORDE and BOSS boards — the waves cleared, the time, the name shown, a platform tag and the time it arrived — under your BOARD ID: the identifier of an anonymous account the game creates for itself, with no email, password or name. Anyone can see these entries. A player the store reports as under 13 sends nothing to the boards.

Deleted automatically. Entries on a daily board are deleted 30 days after the day closes, on a weekly board 90 days after the week closes, and on a monthly board 365 days after the month closes; reports of names are deleted about 90 days after they are made. The database does this by itself, usually within a day of the date. Entries on the all-time boards, the anonymous account, and entries and reports made before 7 October 2026 are kept until deleted on request.

Deleted on request, sooner. To have everything removed now:

  1. Open Hordelane and go to the Survival page.
  2. Tap PERSONAL RECORDS. The line near the bottom reads BOARD ID, followed by a long code of letters and digits. It cannot be copied by tapping, so write it down exactly or take a screenshot. If there is no such line, the game has never sent anything to the boards, or for invites, from this installation of the game.
  3. Email it to playrg.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my board entries. One email can carry both your DATA ID and your BOARD ID.

Because BOARD IDs and codes can be seen by others, the developer may ask you to confirm the request from your phone, for example with a screenshot of PERSONAL RECORDS or INVITE & GET.

What is deleted: your entries on every HORDE and BOSS board — every day, week and month since the boards began, and all time — the reports you sent about other players' names, the reports other players sent about yours, your invite records (as described below), and the anonymous account itself. Requests are handled by one person, so allow a few days for a reply; it is done within 30 days of the request, and you will get an email confirming it.

What is not: anything kept on devices rather than on the boards' server. Other players' phones may still list your BOARD ID among the players they hid. Your own saved game, and on Android your Play Games backup if you use one, may still hold, for each HORDE daily, weekly or monthly board your result was entered on, a note of the board, the BOARD ID and the score sent, until the game has checked that board for a prize after it closes. None of that is shown to anyone. The name you chose with CHANGE NAME also stays in your saved game and backup, and goes onto any new entries you make; change it with CHANGE NAME if you want a different one. Google keeps its own records of requests to its servers under its own rules.

The game keeps the BOARD ID on your device and does not restore it from a Play Games backup, so note it before you uninstall. If you play Survival again after the deletion, the game creates a new BOARD ID, and new entries go under that one.

Your invite records

INVITE & GET in the lobby keeps a few records on the boards' server, all under your BOARD ID: your six-character code, with your BOARD ID as its owner and the time it was made; the BOARD IDs of the players who joined with your code, and of those who then won stage 10; and, if you entered a friend's code, which code you joined with and when. Anyone who knows a code can read that code's record. No name, contact or message is stored. A player the store reports as under 13 makes none of these records.

On Android, a player who installed the game from a friend's link has the friend's code read from Google Play's install referrer on the phone. That text is not stored by the developer: only the code found in it is sent, as the join above, and it is deleted with it.

Deleted on request. Email your BOARD ID to playrg.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my board entries, as for the boards; one request covers both. The record of the code you joined with is deleted, and your BOARD ID is replaced with the word "deleted" in every code's record that holds it: as the owner of your own code, and in the lists of players who joined or won on other players' codes. The lists are not shortened, because the game counts them to pay other players their rewards; your code, with no owner, can no longer be joined. It is done within 30 days, and you will get an email confirming it.

No BOARD ID to send? The BOARD ID is shown in PERSONAL RECORDS on the Survival page, which opens later in the campaign. Until you can open it, there is a manual route for your invite records:

  1. Open INVITE & GET in the lobby. Your six-character code is shown under YOUR CODE, beside SHARE; if you have none yet, opening the page while connected makes one.
  2. Email that code to playrg.games@gmail.com with the subject Delete my board entries.

The developer reads from your code's record the BOARD ID it was made under and deletes everything held under that BOARD ID, exactly as above. A code made before a reinstall leads to the BOARD ID of that earlier installation.

What is not deleted: your own saved game keeps your code, how many friends it has paid for, the code you joined with and the BOARD ID that recorded your join; on Android your Play Games backup does too, if you use one. They are on your device and in your own Google account, not on the server, and they are removed with the save. Invite records have no automatic deletion date: without a request they are kept for as long as invites run.

Your Play Games backup

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your saved progress — the stage you reached, your coins and diamonds, which survivors and weapons you own, and your invite code — is kept in your own Google account, not on any server belonging to the developer. The developer cannot read it and cannot delete it for you, so emailing the DATA ID does not remove it.

You remove it yourself, the same way you would for any other game: open the Play Games app, go to your profile, find Hordelane, and delete its saved game; or delete the game's data from your Google account settings. Signing in also lets the game see the gamer name and avatar on your Play Games profile. The game shows that name in Settings, so you can see which account your progress is backed up to, and offers it as a suggestion when you open CHANGE NAME for the global boards. It leaves the device only if you press SAVE there; it then becomes your name on the boards and is deleted with your board entries, as described above. It appears in none of the analytics records.
