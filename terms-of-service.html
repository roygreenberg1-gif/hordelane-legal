5. Offers and the piggy bank (Android)

The offers are bundles of in-game items sold for money. Each one has its own card, which shows what it holds and the price Google Play charges, in your currency. Nothing in an offer is random: you get what the card shows. The rules every offer keeps:

  • Timers are real. A timed offer is on sale for 48 hours from the moment its card first appears in the lobby, whether or not you open it, and its card shows the time left. When the time is up it is gone, and it does not come back except as described below.
  • At most one offer opens by itself in the lobby a day. The HELP PACK is the only offer shown outside the lobby: on the summary after a loss, as described below, at most once a day. None opens by itself, and the HELP PACK is never offered, for a player the store's age check reports as under 13, or before the store has answered that check. The piggy bank is not an offer: its own page may open by itself in the lobby when a new piggy arrives and when it is full.
  • STARTER PACK. Offered once you have won stage 6, and once more, for another 48 hours, a week after it was first shown. It can be bought once.
  • HERO OFFER. Offered once, three days after your first purchase with money or your first win of stage 12, whichever comes first. It holds diamonds and the survivor its card names. If you already have that survivor when the purchase is delivered, you get the survivor's price in diamonds instead, and the card says so.
  • WORLD PACK. Offered once when you reach a new world (from the next world you reach after installing version 1.3.2; if you reach more than one before it is shown, it is offered for the latest). Its coins grow with the world it is for.
  • WELCOME BACK. When you come back after seven days or more away, the game gives you a free gift, and, once you have won stage 6, offers WELCOME BACK for 48 hours. Each such return brings a new gift and a new offer.
  • HELP PACK. Offered on the summary after a loss, once you have lost the same campaign stage three times in a row, at most once a day. It is not offered in the first stages, while the game still gives hearts away when they run out.
  • SURVIVOR CARD. On sale, with no timer, whenever no card of yours is running, once you have won stage 6; it never opens by itself. It gives diamonds at once and more diamonds on each of seven days, the day it is delivered being day 1. The days are your phone's calendar days, by the time the game trusts. Each day's diamonds wait on the card until you press COLLECT, and every day's diamonds must be collected by the end of day 7: any day not collected by then is lost when day 8 begins, and is not refunded. One card runs at a time; if a second card is delivered while one is running (for example a payment that completed late), its seven days' diamonds are paid at once.
  • HOLIDAY BUNDLE. Offered during its holiday event (section 3), on the event's own days and not in the claim days after them, once you have won stage 6. Unlike the 48-hour offers, its card shows the time left until the event's last day ends. Its card may open by itself once per event, under the rule above. Each can be bought once per player per event. Nothing in it is random: you get what its card shows. Its coins grow with your progress, as the missions' coins do, and the card shows the coins you get. Black Friday has three, the STARTER, WARLORD and LEGEND STACKs: the WARLORD STACK always includes everything in the STARTER STACK, whether or not you bought the STARTER STACK first, and once you buy it the STARTER STACK is no longer offered.
  • The piggy bank. It appears for four days in every seven, fills with coins while you play, and can be bought once it is full. Once bought, or once its four days are over, it is gone until the next seven-day round begins; a piggy not bought within its four days loses its coins. Its price is set from the coins it holds when full, in fixed steps. Buying it pays the coins it holds at the moment the purchase is delivered. With a payment Google Play holds as pending, that moment comes when Google Play confirms the payment: you get the coins the piggy holds then, which may be more or fewer than when you pressed. If there is no piggy with coins at that moment, the purchase is left open and the game tries again each time it is opened, so it may pay a later piggy's coins instead. A purchase still open three days after its payment completed has paid no coins, and Google Play then refunds it by itself.
